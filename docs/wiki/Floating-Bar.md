# Floating bar

> [Versión en español](Barra-Flotante)

A slim column of buttons that floats above everything else, for the ones you
use most. Open it from the bar button in the title bar and set it up on the
**BAR** screen (how many slots, which button goes in each, size, opacity and
side).

- Each slot **references a deck button**, not a copy: editing or toggling it
  here affects the deck and vice versa.
- **Toggles share their state** between the deck, kiosk mode and the bar: a
  two-state button on in the bar shows on in the other screens.
- **Widgets** (clock, weather, now playing, sensor, variable, currency)
  show live data, like the main grid.
- Accepts **GIFs and images**: their animations don't pause when the window
  is in the background.
- Drag it to move it. Taps and hold behave like the main screen. It has no
  editing panel of its own: to edit, use the deck's editor.
