# Themes

> [Versión en español](Temas)

The theme is chosen under Settings (gear) → **THEME** (also in the first-run tutorial).
Three modes:

- **Dark**: pure OLED black background (`#070809`), surface `#111315`,
  borders `#26292e`. Meant for OLED screens and to avoid glare at night.
- **Light**: matte industrial greys (`#d8dbe0` background, `#cbcfd5` surface,
  `#111418` text). Never pure white, to spare your eyes.
- **System**: follows Windows' light/dark mode.

## Accent

Ten quick colors included, plus a free color picker. The accent applies
everywhere (borders, badges, highlights) and is saved with the profile on
export.

All UI color comes from the active theme: change it once and every screen —
main, editor, settings, kiosk, floating bar and mobile remote — updates.
