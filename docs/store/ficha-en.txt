========================================
PRODUCT NAME
========================================
VirtualDeck

========================================
DESCRIPTION
========================================
VirtualDeck turns any touch screen, tablet or secondary monitor into a button panel for your PC. No hardware to buy.

Every button does something: open a program, switch the audio device, type some text, run a macro, light up your RGB, run a script. You organize them in pages and folders, give them an icon and a color, and they are one tap away.

And it does not live only on the PC screen. With the mobile remote you press the same buttons from your phone's browser, with your theme and your accent color. And if you own a physical Stream Dock, VirtualDeck drives it: its LCD keys are painted with your icons and its buttons and knobs trigger actions, with several pages per device.

WHAT YOU CAN PUT ON A BUTTON

Programs, files and web pages. Keyboard shortcuts and automatic typing. Media control: play, pause, next, volume, mute, shuffle and repeat. Switching the default audio device, which in Windows takes four clicks and here takes one. Monitor brightness. Shut down, restart, sleep and lock. Keyboard and mouse macros that you record once and replay. RGB control through OpenRGB, with 18 presets. PowerShell scripts, with the output saved in a variable if you need it. Counters, timers and buttons that branch depending on the value of a variable.

One icon catalog with search: about 2,900 brands and 5,100 action icons, ready to copy to any button. Animated dot icons: sweep, pulse, blink and scan over any icon.

MORE THAN BUTTONS

A button can show information instead of an icon: the clock, the weather, what is playing with its cover art, the CPU or GPU temperature, the exchange rate of a currency, or the value of your own variables.

And not every button needs a finger. You can make one fire by itself at a given time (choosing the days of the week), or when a sensor crosses a threshold: have the fans speed up when the GPU reaches 80 degrees, or switch to night mode at eleven.

KIOSK MODE

Borderless full screen, so you can leave the tablet on all day acting only as a deck. With an optional PIN so nobody leaves without permission.

FLOATING BAR

A thin strip always visible over your other windows, with the buttons you use most. It takes little space and stays out of the way.

FROM YOUR PHONE AND FROM ANOTHER PC

VirtualDeck starts a local server, only on your network and protected by a token that you generate. With it you can press buttons from your phone's browser or from another computer that also runs VirtualDeck.

YOURS AND PORTABLE

The configuration is a JSON file that you can export, import and take with you. There is a store of ready-made profiles and pages to get started, and before importing any of them you are shown exactly what it does: whether it launches programs, runs scripts or types things.

OLED dark theme and cement light theme, ten accent colors, and the whole app in Spanish and English.

OPEN SOURCE

VirtualDeck is free software under the MIT license. The full code, the change history and the bug tracker are on GitHub.

NOTES

RGB lighting requires installing OpenRGB. Temperature sensors require LibreHardwareMonitor with its web server enabled. Both are free and the app links to their download.

========================================
WHAT'S NEW IN THIS VERSION
========================================
- Native video in the music panel across all layouts: adaptive capture resolution matching on-screen dimensions, reducing CPU usage to one-third without losing crispness. Available in both standard vertical panel and ultrawide bar layout (1280x480).
- Window occlusion detection: automatic visual notification when another window covers the video player in the background.
- Redesigned web showcase and gallery: scroll-driven dot matrix dissolve transition, interactive touch console, bilingual trailer (ES/EN), and high-resolution banner captures.
- Automated vd-core build: low-level native core is automatically compiled and bundled during the store build pipeline.
- Official and exclusive Microsoft Store distribution: seamless and secure updates backed by trusted MSIX packaging and official Windows certification.
